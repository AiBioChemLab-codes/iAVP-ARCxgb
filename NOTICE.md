# Third-party attribution

Parts of the handcrafted sequence-feature implementation in `utils/artfeat/` are based on or adapted from routines and supporting data distributed with iLearnPlus/iFeature.

Relevant references:

1. Chen Z, Zhao P, Li F, et al. iFeature: a Python package and web server for features extraction and selection from protein and peptide sequences. *Bioinformatics*. 2018;34(14):2499-2502.
2. Chen Z, Zhao P, Li C, et al. iLearnPlus: a comprehensive and automated machine-learning platform for nucleic acid and protein sequence analysis, prediction and visualization. *Nucleic Acids Research*. 2021;49(10):e60.

Upstream project:

- iLearnPlus: https://github.com/Superzchen/iLearnPlus

The PyPI distribution of iLearnPlus identifies the project license as MIT. Users redistributing or modifying third-party portions should retain the applicable upstream notices and comply with the corresponding license terms.

The AAIndex- and BLOSUM62-related descriptors also rely on published biochemical and substitution-matrix resources cited in the manuscript.
